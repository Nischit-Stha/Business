ARCHITECTURE SUMMARY:
================================================================================

  Customer uploads photo
    → Converted to 2-5MB Base64
    → Stored in database
    → Slow queries, bloated DB
    → Can't scale

AFTER (Optimized):
  Customer uploads photo

    → Only URL saved in DB (< 1KB)
    → Fast queries, lean DB
    → Scales infinitely
    → Secure with RLS policies
