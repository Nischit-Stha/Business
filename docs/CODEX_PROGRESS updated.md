- **Maintenance history:** Added durable service records with vehicle, typed service, odometer/date, calculated next odometer, optional next date, cost, notes, lifecycle status, performer/creator, and timestamps. Completed records are retained historically. Existing workshop-job completion remains compatible and now also writes the service ledger.
- **Service automation:** Completed scheduled services set the maintenance plan baseline and calculate the next service using the stored per-vehicle interval, defaulting to 10,000 km. Valid manual overrides from 1,000–50,000 km are retained. Exposure is `DUE_SOON` within 1,500 km and `OVERDUE` at or beyond the next-service odometer.
- **Odometer:** Existing trusted pickup, return, service, and manual readings remain the authoritative inputs. They update `vehicles.odometer`, which drives maintenance exposure immediately. Backward readings are rejected transactionally and cannot reduce the current odometer.
- **Compliance automation:** Added derived registration/RWC exposure with 30-, 14-, and 7-day thresholds plus missing and expired states. Active customer licence expiry is tracked from the existing customer field; expired licences on active agreements become high-priority owner attention. General operational views expose only names/statuses/dates—not licence numbers/images, addresses, contacts, document URLs, or private files.
- **Attention lifecycle:** The refresh routine upserts stable deduplicated service, registration, RWC, and licence exceptions, escalates expired vehicle compliance above warnings, retires legacy keys, and automatically resolves attention only after its underlying condition clears. Unchanged conditions update in place rather than producing duplicate open records.
- **Fleet readiness:** Allocation readiness now requires valid registration and RWC, no open workshop job, no in-progress maintenance record, and no existing blocking vehicle issue, in addition to the prior operational-status rules.
- **UI:** The maintenance page now contains due soon, overdue, workshop, scheduled work, and completed history sections; supports scheduling, starting, completing, costing, notes, and interval overrides; and includes direct vehicle links and issue counts. Vehicle detail now shows current/last/next service, remaining or overdue kilometres, full service history, rego/RWC exposure, expiry warnings, and open issue access.
- **Security/audit:** New tables use deny-by-default RLS with active-staff reads and no browser writes. Controlled RPCs repeat active-staff authorization. Service creation, status, completion, interval changes, odometer changes, compliance updates, and automated attention refreshes are attributable in the existing audit ledger.
