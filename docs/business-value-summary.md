# Veera V2 business value

## Time saved

| Old workflow | Veera V2 workflow |
|---|---|
| Check weekly payments manually | Durable weekly obligations, staged reconciliation, allocation and reminders |
| Search messages/spreadsheets for who has a car | Current custody, agreement and movement history in one record |
| Remember service intervals | Odometer-based exposure with 10,000 km default and vehicle overrides |
| Work out who had a car for a toll | Timestamped custody matching with evidence and an ambiguity queue |
| Call customers for routine status | Customer portal for car, payments, service, documents, issues and requests |

## Stress reduced

- Allocation fails closed when approval, documents, compliance, maintenance or issues block readiness.
- Financial and audit history is retained; corrections are compensating records.
- Owner attention prioritises overdue money, unsafe fleet states, compliance, failures and genuine ambiguity.
- Notifications and jobs are retry-safe, deduplicated and visible when they repeatedly fail.

## Scale enabled

- Staff use Today, search, bounded payment queues and exception views instead of scanning entire ledgers.
- Server-controlled workflows preserve the same invariants as fleet/customer volume grows.
- Provider-neutral boundaries allow later RENTA, STARR365, bank, SMS or WhatsApp work without making current operations depend on them.

No specific time or monetary saving is claimed until Veera measures the before/after workflow in a controlled trial.
